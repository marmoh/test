One hero, one finger, three hundred levels.

On a small curtained stage, a paper screen lights up. The Scout, a puppet cut from ink, faces the creatures of the night. Move to dodge: as she walks, she unwinds a thread behind her. Close a loop around your enemies and the Moon Scissors cut them out of the paper in one stroke. Stand still, and her silver needles fire on their own.

CIRCLE, CUT, DODGE
• One finger, no joystick: move from anywhere on the screen.
• Circle a single enemy or a whole group: the more the loop catches, the harder the cut hits, and it wipes out any projectiles caught inside.
• Watch out for the Paper Moth, which nibbles the thread, and the Scissor Crab, which slices it as it charges.
• Every enemy attack is telegraphed: readable, demanding, never unfair.

300 LEVELS, 5 KINGDOMS
• A saga map through Ombrelune Forest, Firefly Swamp, the Amber Desert, the Frost Peaks and the Ashlands.
• Levels of 2 to 4 rooms: between rooms, choose 1 power out of 3 (Steel Thread, Echo, Chain Lightning, Guardian Orb…).
• Stars, hard levels and 5 three-phase bosses, each with its own openings.
• Every level is tested by bots before it reaches you: difficulty rises in waves, with no walls.

A WEEKLY TOURNAMENT
• The same level, the same power offers and the same heroine for everyone.
• 5 attempts per week that can’t be bought. No boosts, no talents: only your skill counts.
• Game Center leaderboard and cosmetic honors.

A FAIR GAME
• No random items, no loot boxes, no gacha currency.
• Lives refill on their own; you only lose one when you’re defeated.
• No ads, no tracking.
• Nothing can be bought for the tournament.

OPTIONAL PURCHASES
• Gems, a one-time Starter Pack and a 30-tier Season Pass whose rewards are shown up front.
• Order of the Dragon, an optional subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 3-day free trial the first time): unlimited lives, 60 gems a week, +25% gold and the Dragon Regalia. No commitment: cancel anytime in your App Store settings, at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
